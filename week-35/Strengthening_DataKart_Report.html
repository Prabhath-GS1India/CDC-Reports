Data notes & assumptions: The ticket export (Zoho Desk, Datakart 2.0 department) does not include a separate “Fixed By” field, so Ticket Owner is used for every ticket regardless of status — including Closed/Fixed tickets — as the closest available proxy for who handled it. Tickets are mapped to a microservice/functional area only where a source Tag exists (trace, reports, mobile); the remaining majority of tickets carry no tag in the source data and are grouped under “General / Unclassified”. Microservice instability scoring is therefore driven primarily by GitHub commit signals (fix-commit ratio and commit volume per repository), supplemented with tagged ticket data where it exists. Person-level charts and tables (commit volume, combined performance, work-type mix) are restricted to the named DataKart Team and Mobile Team members supplied for this review; microservice-level analysis covers all contributors and all tickets.
