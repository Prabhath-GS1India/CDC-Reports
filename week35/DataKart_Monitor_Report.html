Executive Summary

Plain-English overview of DataKart’s infrastructure and code-quality health as of 28-08-2026.

What’s good

  • Cluster CPU utilization averaged 5.3% across 10 nodes over the last 7 days — comfortably within normal range (<60%).
  • Database storage for dk2db-products (47%), dk2db-products-replica1 (46%) and dk2-pgsql (20%) all remain at comfortable levels.
  • SonarQube ‘Hakuna Matata’ codebase closed more issues (37) than it opened (12) in the last 7 days — the backlog is shrinking slightly.
  • No pod out-of-memory restarts were recorded in the most recent snapshot that tracks them (15th–21st Aug: 0 restarts).

What needs attention

  • dk2-tracedb briefly spiked to 97.29% (on 24th Aug) CPU even though its weekly average is only 10.4% — worth checking for a batch job or lock contention.
  • dk2-tracedb storage has climbed for three straight weeks (38% → 51% → 56%) — worth planning capacity ahead of the 70% watch threshold.
  • ClickHouse analytics DB storage is at 59% used (206 GiB free of 497 GiB).
  • Sentry logged 248 error-level and 5 fatal-level issues in the last 7 days — recommend triage, especially the fatal crashes.
  • SonarQube backlog for Current Team is 2,476 open of 2,628 issues (94%, all-time) — recommend a cleanup sprint; this week added a net +40 issues (59 opened vs 19 closed).
  • SonarQube backlog for Hakuna Matata (legacy) is 23,220 open of 23,536 issues (99%, all-time) — still very high despite this week's net-positive flow.

Neutral / FYI

  • The Sentry export used for this report covers roughly the last 7 days only (393 issues seen); 14-day/monthly filters currently show the same figures as 7-day until a longer export is available.
  • Server Details charts (Nodes, CPU/Memory, restarts) are drawn from the weekly infrastructure export because the live dkmon-clickhouse metrics source specified in the brief could not be reached from this environment; true hourly/daily granularity, total pod count and API-request volume are not available in any provided source and are labelled as such below rather than estimated.
  • The dk2blob storage account has grown steadily from 8.77 TB to 9.91 TB since 9th May — a slow, predictable trend rather than a sudden change.
